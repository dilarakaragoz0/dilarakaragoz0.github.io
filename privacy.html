Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Lilyish Yayin ("the app", "we") handles information when you use it.

1. What the app does

Lilyish Yayin uploads videos to a YouTube channel on behalf of the user who signs in with Google and grants permission. It uses YouTube API Services.

2. Information we access

  • Google account authorization: When you sign in, Google issues an OAuth access token and refresh token that let the app act on your YouTube channel with the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.
  • YouTube data: The app uses this access only to upload videos and set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and privacy status, and to read basic channel and video information needed to confirm an upload succeeded.

The app does not access your email, contacts, files or any other Google data.

3. How information is stored

OAuth tokens are stored only on the device or server where the app runs, so the app can continue uploading without asking you to sign in each time. We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. YouTube API Services and Google

By using this app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Your data is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

Lilyish Yayin's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account security settings at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. When access is revoked, stored tokens stop working; you may also ask us by email to delete any stored tokens, and we will do so within 7 days.

6. Cookies

This website and the app do not use cookies or tracking technologies.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
